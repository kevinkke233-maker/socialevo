How to play

01

Your first steps

Your task is to help revise and submit the team’s competition report. Read the opening dialogue and reply when invited. Use Read the Starting scope note to inspect the project on the shared computer, then follow Current action to join the group discussion. Review the actual entry record when prompted and confirm when you are ready to begin the report review. You may explore or talk first; meeting every member is not required.

B
02

Move, read and reply

Hold WASD or the arrow keys, or click a clear spot to move. Approach a member and press E, or click their character/name. Click Continue or press Space for the next line. When a reply is requested, select an option or write your own words, then use Confirm reply or Send. Choosing a conversation topic only fills a draft.

WASD / ↑ ↓ ← → · E · Space
03

Use the shared computer

Press B or click Shared computer. Work pack contains the starting scope, draft and anonymous scores. When the report editor is available in Current, edit its text there. Submissions contains versions you actually sent and the feedback you received. Close the computer to return to the room and continue a conversation.

B
04

Save, review and formally submit

Save draft keeps your writing; it does not send it to anyone. Send this report for review sends the current text to the advisors. Read the feedback and, after further edits, send the changed version again. Formally submit this report is a separate action: use it when it becomes available and you are ready to enter the competition. A review submission is not the competition entry.

05

If you are unsure what to do next

Check Current action at the upper left, or press J and open Current action in Notes. Follow the displayed instruction to read a document, finish a reply, review feedback or confirm a discussion. If a button is unavailable, read its nearby explanation. H reopens this guide. You can choose whom to talk to; there is no requirement to ask everyone or exhaust every topic.

J / H
06

The deadline and the end of a session

The competition deadline is the end of the seventh working day in the story; you do not wait seven real days. Conversations and work in the story advance its calendar. Time you spend reading, typing or waiting for replies is not charged separately. A new day does not complete the report for you. Keep following Current action and later confirmations until the end-of-session prompt. Then follow the page to answer or skip the short form, view the full recap and confirm before continuing.
