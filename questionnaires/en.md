# Lab Game Experience and New Situations Questionnaire

This is the complete blank questionnaire. It collects or transmits no answers and includes no delayed follow-up. Use the assigned X→Y or Y→X order; each person completes only one full schedule, not both.

There are 59 distinct item templates. Repeating the brief per-game ratings and confidence ratings at their scheduled times produces 66 response positions per order. Questions may be skipped. The two complete orders are shown separately; repeated content does not add new questions.

Game A is the game played first and Game B is the game played second; these labels do not name fixed game versions. X/Y are the two new-situation forms, separate from games A/B. Provide only one situation form at each pre/post assessment, keep the other form out of view until its scheduled time, and do not provide model answers.

## Assessment order X→Y — 66 response positions

### Five assessment times

1. Before either game: complete the 4 written-response positions in form X, then the 3 confidence items.
2. After game A: complete the 4 per-game character ratings first, then view the complete game A recap.
3. After acknowledging the complete game A recap: complete the 4 written-response positions in form Y, then the 3 confidence items.
4. After game B: complete the 4 per-game character ratings first, then view the complete game B recap.
5. After acknowledging the complete game B recap: complete the 44-item comparison (40 comparison statements, 1 overall choice, and 3 optional open-ended questions).
