# 完整中英文问卷 / Complete bilingual questionnaire

- 中文：[Markdown](zh-CN.md) · [可离线打开及打印的 HTML](zh-CN.html)
- English: [Markdown](en.md) · [Offline, print-friendly HTML](en.html)

这是未填写的去回访问卷材料。共有59个不同模板；按流程重复单局简表和信心短表后，每个填写顺序有66个作答位置。每份文档分别完整列出 X→Y、Y→X；每人只使用已安排的一种顺序，不需要作答两份。HTML 默认展开 X→Y，可收起它并展开 Y→X；打印前只保留所选顺序展开。

This is a blank questionnaire without delayed follow-up. There are 59 distinct templates and 66 response positions per schedule after repeating the per-game and confidence ratings. Each document includes complete X→Y and Y→X schedules; use only the assigned one. HTML opens with X→Y expanded. Close it and open Y→X when needed; leave only the assigned order open before printing.

填写安排：游戏前7个位置 → A结束后、回顾前4个 → 确认A完整回顾后7个 → B结束后、回顾前4个 → 确认B完整回顾后44个。A/B表示游玩先后；X/Y表示情境表。实际填写时分时点提供，不提前展示后测情境。所有题目允许跳过。

Schedule: 7 positions before either game → 4 after A, before its recap → 7 after acknowledging A's complete recap → 4 after B, before its recap → 44 after acknowledging B's complete recap. A/B indicate play order; X/Y identify situation forms. Provide sections at their scheduled times and keep later situation forms out of view until then. Questions may be skipped.

这些文件没有填写内容或提交接口；无需联网即可查看。打印或另存 PDF 可使用浏览器的打印功能。

These files contain no completed answers or submission endpoint and can be viewed offline. Use the browser's print function to print or save as PDF.
